How a session works

Three rules, and the app is built entirely out of them.

The gap is the point

Distraction costs rewards, never time

Twenty seconds in another window and earning freezes. Come back and it resumes. The session itself runs to the end either way, so quitting early is never the cheaper option.

Before 30 minutes

Leave early and the session is lost

Coins, eggs and Pips from that session go with it. Past thirty minutes you keep half the coins and two Pips. Anything already in your garden is never touched.

Afterwards

Eggs hatch on your breaks

Never mid-work-block. Twenty species across six rarities, and the ones you bring into a session earn alongside you in a small garden under the timer.
